🔑 Log in / Sign up

No password needed – we email you a sign-in link. New here? The same link creates your account.

How it works
  1. Enter your email and tap Email me a sign-in link.
  2. You'll get an email from Supabase Auth (noreply@mail.app.supabase.io) called “Your sign-in link”.
  3. Open it and tap Sign in – you come straight back here, logged in.
Not there in a minute? Look in Spam / Promotions. The link works once and expires soon.
